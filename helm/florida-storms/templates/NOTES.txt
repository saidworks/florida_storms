Florida Storms has been deployed!

To access the application:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "florida-storms.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Then open:
  Swagger UI: http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/swagger-ui/index.html
  Health:     http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/actuator/health

To evict all caches:
  curl -X DELETE http://localhost:{{ .Values.service.port }}/admin/cache
